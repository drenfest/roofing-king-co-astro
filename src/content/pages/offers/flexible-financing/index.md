<h2>Flexible Financing with Hearth</h2>
<p class="lead ">Roof King &amp; Restoration in Columbus offers flexible financing options for the roof you need now, with monthly payments that make sense for your budget.</p>
</div>
</div></div><div class="rk-blog-lede-media"><div class="container container-page">
<div class="row">
<div class="col-md-6 format-page">
<div class="offer-hero">
            <img class="img-responsive lazyload" src="/media/wordpress/2de5dafcc2af-bba2f82b-eb69-4131-9c0a-a2e37d69fc01.jpg" alt="Flexible Financing with Hearth">
        </div>
<p>A new roof is a big investment, but it shouldn’t be a stressful one. That’s why Roof King &amp; Restoration partners with Hearth to help homeowners find simple, predictable financing for roofing and exterior projects. Hearth connects you with multiple trusted lending partners through one quick application, helping you compare monthly payment options and choose what fits best.</p>
<p>With Hearth financing, you can move forward on your timeline, without tapping into your home equity or delaying needed work.</p>
<h2>How it Works</h2>
<p><strong>Pay for your project with predictable monthly payments, without using home equity</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Check rates in less than 2 minutes</strong></p>
<p>Fill out a fast, secure form to see personalized offers from Hearth’s lending partners. This step is a soft check and does <strong>not</strong> affect your credit score.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Compare monthly payment options</strong></p>
<p>Review rates and terms side-by-side from over a dozen lenders, then pick the loan that works best for you.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Finalize your application</strong></p>
<p>Verify your information with the lender you choose and complete the approval process online.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Get funded and schedule your project</strong></p>
<p>Loans typically fund in <strong>1 to 5 business days</strong>, and some customers may receive funds as soon as 24 hours after approval.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h2>Why Homeowners Love Hearth Financing</h2>
<p></p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Fast, secure approvals</strong></p>
<p>See your rate options quickly, without a hard credit hit upfront.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Fixed monthly payments</strong></p>
<p>No surprises. Hearth loans come with fixed rates and fixed terms, so you know your payment amount from day one.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Get your money fast</strong></p>
<p>Funding is usually 1 to 5 days on average, letting you start your roof project sooner.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>No prepayment penalties</strong></p>
<p>Pay off your loan early anytime, with no extra fees.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Wide range of loan amounts and terms</strong></p>
<p>Hearth partners may offer loans from $1,000 to $250,000 with terms ranging roughly 2 to 12 years depending on approval.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p></p>
<p>Ready to find a payment plan that works for you? Hearth makes it easy to compare your options and move forward with confidence.</p>
