<h2>Why Choose the GAF System Plus Warranty?</h2>
<p>The GAF System Plus Warranty is designed to provide long-term coverage for your roof, giving you the security you deserve.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>50-Year Coverage:</strong> Full material warranty for up to 50 years.</li>
<li><strong>Labor and Materials:</strong> Covers both the materials and workmanship for added protection.</li>
<li><strong>GAF Certified:</strong> Installed by <a href="/roofing/gaf/">GAF-certified roofing professionals</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Enhanced Roof Protection:</strong> Includes additional coverage for specific roof system components.</li>
<li><strong>Non-Prorated Protection:</strong> Coverage is not reduced over time for the first 10 years.</li>
</ul>
<p>With the GAF System Plus Warranty, you can trust that your roof is built to last, with full coverage to back it up.</p>
