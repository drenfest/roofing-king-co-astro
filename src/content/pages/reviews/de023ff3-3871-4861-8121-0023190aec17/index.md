<p class="review-body">After receiving 31 estimates<br>
I had an outstanding experience with RoofKing from start to finish. The entire team was professional, responsive, and efficient, but what really stood out was the incredible service provided by their sales manager, Cosmo. He was knowledgeable, patient, and took the time to walk me through every detail of the process, making sure I felt comfortable and informed at each step.</p>
<p>Cosmo’s professionalism and fairness made a big impression—he wasn’t pushy at all, just genuinely helpful and honest. It’s rare to come across someone so committed to doing right by the customer. If you’re considering a new roof or repairs, I can’t recommend RoofKing—and especially Cosmo—highly enough. Let’s not forget project manager Kyle, he was great after they finished the roof they cleaned up all over the yard<br>
I am not an easy client to deal with and if I take my time to write a review ( using chat gpt lol)shows how good they are</p>
