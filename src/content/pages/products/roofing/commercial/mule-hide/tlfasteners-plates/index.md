<article><h1>Mule-Hide TL Fasteners &amp; Plates</h1><p>TL Fastener is designed to mechanically attach rigid insulation board and single-ply membrane to structural cement wood fiber, gypsum and lightweight insulating concrete roof decks. Features a high-thread profile with a tapered root diameter and sharp point. Its unique design allows it to penetrate the roof deck and progressively compact the base material, creating a stronger hold in weak material. Used in conjunction with the 2" TL Seam Plate and 3" TL Insulation Plate.</p></article>
