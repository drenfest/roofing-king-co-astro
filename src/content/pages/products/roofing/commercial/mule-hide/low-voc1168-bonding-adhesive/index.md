<article><h1>Mule-Hide Low-VOC 1168 Bonding Adhesive</h1><p>This version meets requirements of California Rule 1168. A high strength solvent-based contact adhesive that allows bonding of EPDM and TPO to various porous and non-porous substrates. Specially formulated using a blend of VOC exempt and non-exempt solvents to be meet the &lt; 250 g/L VOC content requirements of the OTC Model Rule for adhesives and sealants.</p></article>
