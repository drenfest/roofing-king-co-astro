<article><h1>Mule-Hide Splice Adhesive</h1><p>Used on EPDM membranes for splicing field sheets. Also used to splice uncured EPDM flashing material to cured EPDM membranes. Use White Splice Adhesive when a white (white-on-black)membrane is required. MuleHide Tape Primer must be used to prepare the membrane prior to the application of the adhesive. This product cannot be used on warranted projects.</p></article>
