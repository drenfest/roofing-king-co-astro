<article><h1>Mule-Hide 702LV Adhesive</h1><p>CCW-702LV is a Low-VOC solvent-based, high-tack adhesives specifically designed to promote maximum adhesion of MuleHide F5 Air and Vapor Barrier/Temporary Roof to approved substrates.
