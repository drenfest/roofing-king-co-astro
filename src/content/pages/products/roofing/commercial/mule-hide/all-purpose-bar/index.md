<article><h1>Mule-Hide All Purpose Bar</h1><p>All Purpose Bar is designed for use as an anchor bar for attachment of MuleHide EPDM membranes. May be installed in the field seam as a batten bar and used at the base of curbs, parapet walls and angle changes as an anchor bar. Also be used with any MuleHide single-ply membranes as a termination bar. Refer to the MuleHide Single-Ply Manuals for specific use and installation instructions.</p></article>
