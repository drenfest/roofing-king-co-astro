<article><h1>Mule-Hide 6" TPO Pressure-Sensitive RUSS</h1><p>May be used with TPO and should only be installed on horizontal surfaces using an appropriate MuleHide fastener below the roof membrane providing additional securement at angle changes. RUSS is secured with MuleHide EHD (#15) or HDP (#14) Fasteners and MuleHide 2.4" Seam Plates. This product requires the use of TPO Tape Primer or Low VOC Tape Primer.</p></article>
