<article><h1>Mule-Hide White-on-Black EPDM</h1><p>Our White-on-Black 60-mil and 90-mil EPDM membranes are used primarily in fully adhered roofing systems on new construction, reroofing and recover (retrofit) applications. The 60-mil membrane may also be used as flexible membrane flashings for walls, curbs, etc, when installing an EPDM membrane roofing systems. The system must be installed over acceptable roof insulation or other suitable substrate. See our MuleHide Specifications Manual for complete specifications and details.</p></article>
