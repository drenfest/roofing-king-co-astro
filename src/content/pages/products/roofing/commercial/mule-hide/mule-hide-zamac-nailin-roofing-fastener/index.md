<article><h1>Mule-Hide MuleHide Zamac Nailin Roofing Fastener</h1><p>The MuleHide Zamac Nailin™ Roofing Fastener is a nail-drive, general purpose anchor that can be used in concrete, block, brick, or stone. Applications include attachment of roof membrane terminations, roof flashing, and mechanical/electrical fixtures. It features an anchor body formed from a corrosion resistant zamac alloy with a carbon steel drive nail. The mushroom head provides a tamperproof installation. Note: The Zamac Nailin Roofing Fastener is not recommended for overhead, life-safety or sustained tensile loading applications unless special considerations are given to the allowable loads.</p></article>
