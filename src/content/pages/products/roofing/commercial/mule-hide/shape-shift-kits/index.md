<article><h1>Mule-Hide ShapeShift® Kits</h1><p>ShapeShift® Kits put everything needed to create pitch pans in one convenient package. The “grab-and-go” kits are available in two sizes to accommodate jobs of various sizes. Large kits contain 16 ShapeShift corner sections, 16 ShapeShift straight sections, 3 - 2 liter pouches of MP Liquid Sealant in white and three cartridges of JTS 1 joint and termination sealant in white. Large kits create four 7- by 7-inch pitch pans. Small kits contain 16 ShapeShift corner sections, 1 - 2 liter pouch of MP Liquid Sealant in white and two cartridges of JTS 1 in white. Small kits create four 4- by 4-inch pitch pans. ShapeShift kits can be used to create pitch pans for acrylic-coated, smooth modified bitumen, and smooth built-up roofing systems. ShapeShift pitch pans are approved for installation on MuleHide warranted projects when installed on vertical surfaces if filled with JTS 1 Joint &amp; Termination Sealant instead of MP Liquid Sealant.</p></article>
