<article><h1>Mule-Hide TPO with Seam Protector</h1><p>TPO with Seam Protector is available on standard TPO membranes in a variety of thicknesses and widths. The Seam Protector film keeps factory seam areas clean during installation, saving crews time and reducing the need for weathered membrane cleaner, rags, or wipes. It is compatible with both fully adhered and mechanically attached systems and helps minimize the risk of cold welds by maintaining cleaner seam areas.</p></article>
