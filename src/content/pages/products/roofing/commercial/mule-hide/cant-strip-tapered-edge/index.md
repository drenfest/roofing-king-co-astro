<article><h1>Mule-Hide Cant Strip &amp; Tapered Edge</h1><p>Cant Strip and Tapered Edge are widely accepted, high quality low-slope roofing products. Each is durable, lightweight and environmentally friendly. These components are factory fabricated from Blue Ridge Fiberboard Structodek insulation board. The Cant Strips are cut at angles that provide a true 45-degree angle between horizontal and vertical surfaces. The Tapered Edge is cut on angles that slope from zero on one edge to ½” to 2″ thickness on the opposite edge.</p></article>
