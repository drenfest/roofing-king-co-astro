<article><h1>Mule-Hide Cantilever Coping</h1><p>Cantilever Coping is the perfect solution for attachment to a parapet wall with a non-structural exterior building facade. Conditions such as EIFS, brick or panels don’t allow for traditional anchoring to the exterior building structure, but Cantilever Coping’s unique support brackets bring extra stability to the roof edge system. It has a full snap-on design, galvanized steel anchor clips and factory-applied stainless steel springs that ensure long-term positive attachment. Cantilever Coping is custom fabricated in sizes to meet specific job requirements.</p></article>
