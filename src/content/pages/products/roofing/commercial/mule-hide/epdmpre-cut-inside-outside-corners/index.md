<article><h1>Mule-Hide EPDM Pre-Cut Inside/Outside Corners</h1><p>Ideal for installing inside and outside corners. Consists of .060 inch (1.5-mm) thick uncured EPDM flashing material laminated to cured butyl rubber tape and rolled onto a poly release liner providing .090 mils (2.25-mm) of total thickness. This product requires the use of Tape Primer or Low-VOC Tape Primer.</p></article>
