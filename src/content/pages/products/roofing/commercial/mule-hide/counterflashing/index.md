<article><h1>Mule-Hide Counterflashing</h1><p>Counterflashing provides a watertight termination at the leading edge of the roofing material. Available in 1 piece reglet, 1-piece surface mounted or 2-piece reglet. - 1 piece reglet is a great option when a receiver is not required. - 1-piece surface mounted is a great option when a receiver and a joint reglet are not required. - 2-piece reglet is easily installed by hooking and rotating the flashing to snap into the receiver.
