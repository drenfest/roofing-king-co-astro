<article><h1>Explore composite decking.</h1><p>Trex offers composite decking with coordinating railing and outdoor-living products. Compare decking collections by appearance, surface texture and the details that matter for how you use the space. Plan the boards, framing, fastening, stairs and railings together rather than selecting the deck surface in isolation.</p><h2>Trex</h2><p>Explore the brand’s material options, design considerations and documentation to discuss with Roof King.</p><a href="/products/decking/composite/trex/">Explore Trex</a><h2>Questions to resolve before choosing</h2><h3>Compare decking collections</h3><p>Review the current Trex collections and physical samples for color and grain. Consider the deck’s sun exposure, barefoot use, furniture and traffic. Surface temperature and the way a board looks outdoors are useful questions to resolve before choosing a color.</p><h3>Complete the outdoor space</h3><p>Coordinate railing, stair details and lighting with the deck layout. Confirm which accessories suit the selected system and how they attach. The framing and support structure need their own assessment; new boards alone do not establish that an existing deck is sound.</p><h3>Care and product documentation</h3><p>Check cleaning guidance, required board spacing, approved fasteners and the exact product warranty. Composite decking has different care requirements from wood. Do not transfer a warranty length or performance claim from one collection to another.</p><p><a href="https://www.trex.com/products/decking/">Manufacturer product information</a></p></article>
