<article><h1>Ply Gem Performance Board + Batten</h1><p>Performance Board + Batten creates vertical lines with a 5½-inch board face and a 1½-inch batten. It can emphasize the height of a gable or become the main exterior pattern. The .048-inch nominal panel thickness and 9⁄16-inch profile distinguish it from the horizontal lap products in the collection.</p><p>Ply Gem lists a 10-foot panel length. On taller walls, horizontal breaks and transitions need to be designed rather than left as an incidental seam. The way the vertical panels meet a roofline, window head or lower siding material affects both the appearance and the drainage detail.</p><p>Starting and finishing a vertical panel is different from starting horizontal lap siding. Match the base channel, corners and opening details to this panel orientation. Review the color on a physical sample, especially where board-and-batten will sit beside another siding profile.</p></article>
