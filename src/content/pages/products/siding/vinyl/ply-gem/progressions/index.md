<article><h1>Ply Gem Progressions</h1><p>Progressions combines a fine-grained, painted-wood appearance with .042-inch nominal-thickness vinyl panels. Its ½-inch profile gives the courses definition without using the deeper profile of Dimensions. It is a useful family to compare when you want several lap-width choices in one collection.</p><p>The four profiles include Double 4 and Double 5 traditional lap plus Double 4½ and Double 5 Dutch lap. A wider exposure changes the number of horizontal lines across the wall; Dutch lap adds a beveled shadow at the top of each course. Those differences are more useful to judge on a full sample than on a small color chip.</p><p>Panel length and color selection vary by profile. Plan the preferred exposure, seams and corner details together, then compare the exact product in the installation estimate. Darker colors are not necessarily available across every Progressions profile.</p></article>
