<article><h1>Ply Gem Transformations</h1><p>Transformations is the .040-inch nominal-thickness lap siding family in the Ply Gem Performance Collection. A ½-inch profile and defined locking system provide a familiar clapboard or Dutch-lap appearance. Its smaller family color palette makes it a straightforward starting point for a conventional exterior.</p><p>Choose Double 4 or Double 5 traditional lap for flat course lines, or Double 4½ Dutch lap for a beveled shadow. These options differ in exposure and panel length, so the profile should be identified by name in the product selection rather than described only as vinyl siding.</p><p>Discuss wall preparation, flashing, trim and fastening alongside the panel. A lower nominal thickness does not remove those installation steps. The finished result depends on the complete wall assembly, including the condition behind the old siding and how the new panels connect at openings and edges.</p></article>
