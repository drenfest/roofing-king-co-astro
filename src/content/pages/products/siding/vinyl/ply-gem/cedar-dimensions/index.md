<article><h1>Ply Gem Cedar Dimensions</h1><p>Cedar Dimensions uses molded polypropylene panels to create the irregular edges and grain associated with cedar shingles and shakes. The family includes straight shingle, hand-split shake and half-round looks, so it can be used to change the character of a gable, porch or full wall rather than simply change its color.</p><p>The profiles are separate panels with their own dimensions and matching corner pieces. Nominal thickness ranges from .080 to .100 inch across the family. A Double 7 shingle and a Double 9 shake should not be treated as interchangeable when calculating quantities or ordering trim.</p><p>Choose the panel profile before the accessories. Ply Gem lists dedicated cedar-panel starter strips and profile-specific corners; those details give the wall a more convincing finish than using an unrelated lap-siding accessory. Color availability also varies by profile.</p></article>
