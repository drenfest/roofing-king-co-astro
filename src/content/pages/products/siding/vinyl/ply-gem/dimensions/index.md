<article><h1>Ply Gem Dimensions</h1><p>Dimensions is the .044-inch nominal-thickness lap siding in the Ply Gem Performance Collection. Its ⅝-inch profile creates a deeper shadow line, making the individual courses more defined across a broad wall. The folded nail hem and oversized lock are parts of the panel design rather than added accessories.</p><p>Choose Double 4 traditional lap for a straightforward clapboard look or Double 4½ Dutch lap for a beveled course with a more pronounced shadow. The profiles have different panel lengths, which matters when planning seams around windows, corners and long wall runs.</p><p>Compare a full sample against the trim and roof color outdoors. The family palette includes solid and cedar-inspired finishes, but the chosen color still needs to be checked against the exact panel profile. Thickness alone is not a whole-wall wind rating; fastening and the supporting wall also matter.</p></article>
