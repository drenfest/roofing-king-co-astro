Even if you know you want asphalt shingles installed, the question becomes, which brand is best?</span><br></p><p>Roof King &amp; Restoration is a <a href="/roofing/">trusted Columbus roofing company</a> that provides a wide range of solutions, from roof repairs to replacements, residential to commercial, gutters, storm damage and more. Special features, including GAF's LayerLock, Dura Grip and and StainGuard technologies help these products adhere tightly to each other and the roof itself, as well as protect against excess moisture and potential water damage. GAF also manufactures its roofing materials to meet the highest possible Class 4 impact rating, as well as a Class A fire rating for the ultimate peace of mind.</p><p>This means that GAF products can perform well through a variety of extreme weather events, including:</p></div><div class="rk-blog-component-card-list"><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>High Wind Speeds<br></div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Hailstorms</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Torrential Rains</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Intense Heat and Sunshine</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Below-Freezing Temperatures</div></article></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Cost Effectiveness</h2><p>GAF shingles are also considered cost-efficient, making them an excellent option for any <a href="/roofing/replacement/">Columbus roof replacement</a>. Even though their products offer a top-shelf performance, GAF keeps its prices competitive and customer-driven.</p><p>Depending on what's best for your home and budget, GAF offers a wide variety of products to accommodate different needs or preferences. For the most affordability, you can shop a variety of classic 3-tab options, all coming with reliable weather resistance and decades-long protection. On the other hand, GAF also manufactures premium designer shingle lines for those interested in a luxury option.</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Stunning and Stylish</h2><p>Homeowners that get a roof replacement featuring GAF shingles aren't just signing up for improved durability and long-lasting performance—these products also offer a beautiful aesthetic. The brand's Designer Shingle Series, for example, come in a variety of styles, including traditional, architectural and premium, and can mimic the look of other roofing materials, such as wood or slate, contributing to the curb appeal at any address.</p><p>GAF also manufactures a wide range of color options. This way, homeowners are able to perfectly complement their home and property by selecting the perfect color for their new roof replacement.</p><p>In order to get these elite benefits out of your roof replacement, it's important to work with a knowledgeable <a href="/roofing/gaf/">Columbus GAF roofer</a>. Roof King has been recognized by the brand for the quality of our installations that feature their materials, which lets our customers know that we can offer superior craftsmanship and ensure they receive all the aforementioned advantages out of their next roof replacement.</p><p>Get in touch with Roof King today for more information, and we'll give you a free estimate on a complete roof replacement featuring premium GAF materials.</p></div></div></section>            </main>
