                <section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-lede-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-lede-layout"><div class="rk-blog-lede-copy"><p>When storm season arrives in your area, it's important to know the signs of damage to your roof. Your roof takes the brunt of the force from a storm, so you must ensure it's in tip-top shape.</p><p>As an experienced Columbus roofing company, we have helped repair numerous storm damages for our customers. This article will cover how to spot roofing damage during this storm season.&nbsp;</p></div><div class="rk-blog-lede-media"><div class="se-component se-image-container __se__float-none"><figure><img src="/media/wordpress/1e3326e5b779-7e23206c-ef6e-43d1-a588-ff8c7a8dafe9.jpg" alt="Roof damage Inspection" class="img-responsive"></figure></div></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Missing or Damaged Shingles&nbsp;</h2><p>The shingles of your roofing system will give you a clear picture of the damage it sustained. Broken or missing shingles are an obvious sign that your roof needs a repair service.</p><p>You can spot these signs of damage by walking around your property and looking for pieces of broken shingles in the grass. These broken shingles can lead your property to deal with leaks and structural problems.</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Water Stains Indoors</h2><p>Water stains on the ceiling or walls are a sign that your roofing system is experiencing a leak. Brownish or yellow spots are generally a sign that water is dripping into the home and then drying.&nbsp;</p><p>When you spot water stains, you should not hesitate to schedule an inspection for the roof. The longer you wait, the greater the chance of a costly repair for your roofing system.</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Sagging Roofing Sections</h2><p>If you spot a sagging roofing section, this requires immediate assistance from a reputable Columbus GAF roofer. A sagging roof means there are structural problems caused by continuous water exposure.&nbsp;</p><p>Once the roof reaches a point where it's sagging, the underlying decking and supports are compromised. If left unfixed, it could lead to a collapse of the roofing system.&nbsp;</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Overflowing Gutters</h2><p>When gutters fill with broken shingle fragments and granules, they can start to clog. After a severe storm, you should check your gutters for any signs of debris from your roofing system.&nbsp;</p><p>Things like shingle granules in a gutter mean it is deteriorating and needs repair. Additionally, having a gutter overflow can also mean there is a problem with the flashing.&nbsp;</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Request a Free Roofing Service Estimate Today</h2><p>Roof King &amp; Restoration is a <a href="/roofing/">professional roofing company</a> helping Ohio homeowners. If you want to ensure that your roof is ready for the storm season, don't hesitate to work with our crews.</p><p>Contact us today to request a free pricing estimate and learn more about our service options. Call to speak with one of our friendly representatives or fill out the online quote form to get started.&nbsp;</p></div></div></section>            </main>
