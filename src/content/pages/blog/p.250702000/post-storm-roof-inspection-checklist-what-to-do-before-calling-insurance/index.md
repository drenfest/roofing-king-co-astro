                <section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-lede-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-lede-layout"><div class="rk-blog-lede-copy"><p>Many areas of the Midwest are projected to see a stormier summer than usual in 2025, and Columbus is no exception. Situated along the Scioto River in Central Ohio, the state's capital experiences its fair share of severe storms throughout the year, but summer tends to bring the most volatile weather. From high winds to hailstorms to heavy rain, there are plenty of elemental conditions that can leave homes damaged in their wake.</p><p>As a trusted Columbus roofing company, Roof King &amp; Restoration encourages customers to familiarize themselves with the post-storm roof restoration process in the event that their homes sustain damage from one of the summer storms projected to blow through the area. Below are the first steps homeowners should take if they suspect their roofs have been damaged by the elements.</p></div><div class="rk-blog-lede-media"><div class="se-component se-image-container __se__float-none"><figure><img src="/media/wordpress/0725022ab2cd-7a7aae39-c6de-43ee-8835-4851fb6946f3.jpg" alt="Post-Roof Inspection" class="img-responsive"></figure></div></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Double-Check Your Insurance Policy</h2><p>Most homeowner insurance policies outline the required steps to take in order to receive coverage for Columbus roof repairs following a storm. We suggest taking a look at your insurance policy ahead of time so that you'll know what you need to do in the event that your home is damaged. You'll likely find information like:</p></div><div class="rk-blog-component-card-list"><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Duties After Loss:</h3><div>This section of your policy will detail everything you need to accomplish, from notifying your provider to protecting your property from further damage to scheduling repairs.<br></div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Timely Filing Requirements:</h3><div>Most policies will have a timeframe in which you need to notify your insurer and officially file your claim, which typically ranges from 30 to 90 days.</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Proof of Loss:</h3><div>Your policy will also state what type of documentation you need to submit in order to show that your home sustained damage. This could include photographs, estimates and repair receipts.</div></article></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Snap Photos of Your Roof After a Storm</h2><p>Knowing common signs of storm damage can help you assess the condition of your roof. While issues like vegetation growth or discoloration may be simply due to its age, there are many other problems that indicate elemental damage occurred. After a storm, we recommend looking for and snapping photos of the following 11 most typical signs of storm damage:</p></div><div class="rk-blog-component-card-list"><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Missing Roof Materials</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Curled, Loosened or Cracked Shingles</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Ceiling or Attic Leaks</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Broken Gutters and Downspouts</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Damaged Soffits, Fascia and Flashing</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Sagging or Drooping Structure</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Pockmarked or Dented Materials</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Granule Loss on Shingles</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Debris on Roof</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Exposed Underlayment</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Damaged Roof Vents<br></div></article></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Schedule a Professional Roof Inspection</h2><p>Next up, it's time to contact a trusted <a href="/storm-damage/">storm restoration contractor</a>. Delaying necessary repairs can be catastrophic for homeowners, as it could lead to being denied coverage by insurance companies. Procrastinating proper maintenance can also result in small roofing issues snowballing into larger and more serious deterioration, presenting safety hazards to you and your family.&nbsp;</p><p>An experienced roofing contractor will complete a thorough roof inspection, document all signs of damage, and complete any necessary repairs. Roof King &amp; Restoration can even communicate with your provider on your behalf, making the entire <a href="/storm-damage/insurance-claims/">insurance claim process</a> much easier and less stressful.</p><p>As a Columbus GAF roofer, another one of the many perks of our storm restoration service is the materials we use. GAF manufactures some of the most durable and reliable roofing products available, which offer resistance to high winds, torrential rains, and much more. It also means that a <a href="/roofing/replacement/">Columbus roof replacement</a> with our team will be accompanied by strong warranty protection for the ultimate peace of mind.</p><p>Get in touch with Roof King &amp; Restoration today to receive a free estimate on our roof restoration services, and learn more about all that we can do to help you protect your Columbus home.</p></div></div></section>            </main>
