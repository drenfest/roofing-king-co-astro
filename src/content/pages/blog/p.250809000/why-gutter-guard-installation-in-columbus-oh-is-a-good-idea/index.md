                <section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-lede-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-lede-layout"><div class="rk-blog-lede-copy"><p>When planning a gutter replacement in Columbus, OH, you might be asking yourself, “Are gutter guards worth it?” When investing in new gutters, adding gutter guards is a wise choice.</p><p>In this article, you can find out more about the immediate benefits you can gain when adding gutter guards to your gutter replacement, get answers to common questions about gutter guards, and then find a local gutter company that provides the products and installation service you need!</p></div><div class="rk-blog-lede-media"><div class="se-component se-image-container __se__float-none"><figure><img src="/media/wordpress/95d7f57eb90b-10d9c011-cd81-434e-a44e-38162c2d9531.jpg" alt="Gutter Guards in OH" class="img-responsive"></figure></div></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>5 Benefits of Quality Gutter Guards in Columbus, OH</h2><p>Functional gutters are essential for the protection of your home’s roofing and siding, and they help prevent erosion and damage to your home’s foundation.</p><p>With so much at stake, it makes sense to take every measure to ensure your gutters are working as they should. This is where gutter guards come in. Here are some of the key benefits you will gain:</p><h3>1. Clog Prevention</h3><p>Gutter guards are designed to keep clogs from forming that impede water drainage. This can include leaves, twigs, nests, seeds, and shingle grit.</p><h3>2. Water Damage Defense</h3><p>Keeping your gutters clog-free helps prevent roof water damage, fascia rot, and ensures that water is directed away from the foundation, thereby protecting your home from soil erosion and basement leaks.</p><h3>3. Ice-Dam &amp; Pest Mitigation</h3><p>Gutter guards reduce winter ice dams by preventing clogs that trap snow and sleet. They also keep rodents, birds, and insects from building nests in your gutters, and eventually, making their way into other structures of your home.</p><h3>4. Maintenance Savings</h3><p>You can reduce your time on a ladder cleaning your gutters by 63%, or in other words, save about 4 hours each year on home chores. If you pay for gutter cleaning services, then that’s more money kept in your bank account.</p><h3>5. Insurance &amp; ROI</h3><p>Gutter guards are considered one of the top weather-proofing upgrades for your home. They can help avoid storm damage claims as well. Because of these benefits, you could save on homeowner’s insurance. Plus, the money saved on exterior repairs is a long-term investment that many consider well worth the initial cost.</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Gutter Guard FAQs</h2><p><span>Even though there are numerous reasons to invest in gutter guards, you want to be fully informed anytime you’re investing in your home. Here are some common questions and answers about gutter guards to help.</span></p><h3>Do Gutter Guards Work During Heavy Rain?</h3><p>When the rain is very heavy and coming down fast, some gutter guards are unable to keep up. When this happens, rain bypasses the gutter entirely. However, this can happen even without gutter guards during intense storms.</p><h3>Can Gutter Guards Save Money?</h3><p>Gutter guards save money in multiple ways, including reducing the need for gutter maintenance, repairs, and replacement. They also help prevent costly damage to your home. It’s essential to weigh the upfront cost of gutter guard installation with the potential for long-term savings.</p><h3>How Long Do Gutter Guards Last?</h3><p>The lifespan of gutter guards depends on the type of guards used and the local climate. For example, aluminum gutter guards can last 10-20 years, while vinyl gutter guards last 2-10 years on average.</p><p>Do you want to learn more about <a href="/gutters/guards/">gutter guards</a>, or are you ready to contact a <a href="/gutters/">local gutter company in Columbus, OH</a>? Reach out to Roof King &amp; Restoration today!</p></div></div></section>            </main>
