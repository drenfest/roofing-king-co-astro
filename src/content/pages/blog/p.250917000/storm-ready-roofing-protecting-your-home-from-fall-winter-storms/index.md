                <section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-lede-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-lede-layout"><div class="rk-blog-lede-copy"><p>Do you live in an area prone to storms from wind, hail, heavy rain, or snow? By conducting detailed inspections and making immediate repairs, protecting your roof is the key to creating a stronger home. You can never be too prepared for fall storms and blizzards, but using these checklists can help.</p></div><div class="rk-blog-lede-media"><div class="se-component se-image-container __se__float-none"><figure><img src="/media/wordpress/7490cd237f0e-a7bf74e9-24d7-460c-92b6-1c62392610a3.jpg" alt="Storm Damage Prevention" class="img-responsive"></figure></div></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Pre-Storm Checklist: Storm Damage Roof Prep</h2><p>The best way to <a href="/storm-damage/">protect your home from storm damage</a> is by taking proactive steps. Each area of your exterior plays a crucial role in defending your home, so addressing small issues ahead of time can save you from costly repairs down the line. Some pre-storm steps you should take include:</p></div><div class="rk-blog-component-card-list"><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Inspecting Shingles:</h3><div>Check the vulnerable areas of your roofing system to make sure they’re ready for fall and winter force by looking for loose, curled, or missing shingles. It helps to schedule a professional roof inspection to spot concerns you may have missed.</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Securing Flashing:</h3><div>Look for gaps around chimneys, vents, and skylights to make sure your home is properly sealed from the weather.</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Clearing Gutters:</h3><div>This is the time to properly clean your gutters and remove leaves and debris that can cause your gutters to back up, freeze, or leak.</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Checking Attic Ventilation:</h3><div>Poor ventilation can cause moisture to build up, leading to winter ice damming.</div></article></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Post-Storm Checklist: How to Protect Your Roof from Storms After Damage</h2><p>It’s important to inspect the condition of your roofing system as soon as it’s safe following a storm. Between fallen trees, heavy snowfall, and ice accumulation, the fall and winter months can slowly weaken your exterior. Take a thorough look around your entire property to spot leaks, missing shingles, and interior stains that can all be signs of further issues. Some key areas to inspect are</p></div><div class="rk-blog-component-card-list"><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Missing Shingles and Granule Loss</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Flashing or Chimney Gaps</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Bent or Clogged Gutters and Downspouts</div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div>Stains on Ceiling or Walls</div></article></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Learn More From a Professional Roofing Contractor in Ohio</h2><p>Don’t let storm damage in Ohio have a lasting effect on your property when you can get professional assistance. Roof King Roofing &amp; Restoration is here to thoroughly defend your property with immediate storm damage protection and <a href="/storm-damage/insurance-claims/">roofing insurance claims assistance</a>. As a local storm damage expert, we’re prepared for the harsh fall and winter weather with comprehensive roofing, repair, gutter, and ventilation services. Begin your fall and winter storm preparations today by scheduling a free inspection.</p></div></div></section>            </main>
