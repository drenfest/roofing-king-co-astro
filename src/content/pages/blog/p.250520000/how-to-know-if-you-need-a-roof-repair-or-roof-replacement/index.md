                <section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-lede-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-lede-layout"><div class="rk-blog-lede-copy"><p>When homeowners start to face roofing issues, they often wonder if a roof repair is enough or if a roof replacement is necessary. The answer to your roofing damage will depend on several factors.&nbsp;</p><p>As an experienced roofer, our team at Roof King &amp; Restoration has handled roof repairs and roof replacements. This article will cover which option is likely the right choice for your situation.&nbsp;</p></div><div class="rk-blog-lede-media"><div class="se-component se-image-container __se__float-none"><figure><img src="/media/wordpress/6b864ae0e9b5-5baca9d6-4a99-4307-b1d3-cf6883c4226a.jpg" alt="repair vs replacement" class="img-responsive"></figure></div></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Age of the Roof</h2><p>Most asphalt roofing systems have a lifespan of 20 to 25 years. If your roofing system is nearing this age, a roof replacement is often a more cost-effective solution.&nbsp;</p><p>You will need to perform repeated repairs and eventually replace the roof if you push the roof replacement to a later date. If you are unsure about the age of your property's roof, a professional inspection can help identify common signs of age.&nbsp;</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Shingle Damage</h2><p>If your roofing system only has a few damaged shingles, it can be repaired without a roof replacement. However, having widespread shingle damage like curling, cracking, or missing pieces is often a sign of a roof replacement.</p><p>Additionally, you should look for shingles that are covered in dark streaks or have granular loss. The dark streaks are often a sign of algae and moisture in the roofing system that requires replacement.&nbsp;</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Water Leaks and Stains&nbsp;</h2><p>A minor hole in a roofing system that leads to a water leak can be patched and restored. However, if you are facing persistent water leaks and stains on your home's ceiling, this is a sign that you need a roof replacement quickly.&nbsp;</p><p>Water intrusion in your home can lead to mold growth and structural problems. If you notice water leaks, you shouldn't hesitate to contact a Columbus roofing company like Roof King &amp; Restoration for help.&nbsp;</p></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Request a Free Roofing Service Estimate Today</h2><p>Delaying a roof repair or roof replacement often means higher costs for you. At Roof King &amp; Restoration, we provide top-rated <a href="/roofing/">roofing services</a> for Columbus, OH and surrounding communities.</p><p>We are a Columbus GAF roofer that will provide a thorough inspection and check to see if a roof repair or roof replacement is necessary for your home. Our team holds itself to strict standards and has earned numerous positive customer reviews.</p><p>Contact us today to request a free pricing estimate and learn more about our service options. Call to speak with one of our friendly representatives or fill out the online quote form to get started.&nbsp;</p></div></div></section>            </main>
