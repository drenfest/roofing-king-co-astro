Our region regularly sees wind gusts topping 40 mph during autumn storms. While that may not sound extreme, those gusts are more than enough to lift loose shingles, expose your roof’s underlayment, or even tear away flashing around chimneys and vents.</p><p>At Roof King, we’ve seen firsthand how quickly a strong windstorm can turn a minor<a href="/roofing/"> roofing</a> issue into a costly repair. Seasonal cold fronts bring rapid pressure changes that whip up short but powerful wind bursts. rk-blog-article-section"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Protect Your Columbus Home with Preventative Roofing Steps</h2><p>When it comes to fall roof maintenance, a little effort goes a long way. We recommend Columbus homeowners take these key steps before peak storm season hits:</p><p>These preventative measures not only save money on future repairs, but also extend the life of your roof.</p></div><div class="rk-blog-component-card-list"><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p><strong>Schedule attic ventilation checks</strong>: Poor ventilation can lead to condensation and weakened roof decking.</p></div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p><strong>Install or upgrade to wind-rated shingles</strong>: Designed to resist uplift, these shingles help prevent future <a href="/storm-damage/">storm damage.</a>​</p></div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p><strong>Secure flashing and seals</strong>: Especially around skylights, vents, and chimneys.</p></div></article><article class="rk-blog-component-card"><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p><strong>Trim back nearby trees</strong>: Prevent large limbs from falling onto your roof during gusty weather.</p></div></article></div></div></section><section class="rk-blog-component-section rk-blog-article-section rk-blog-article-section-narrative"><div class="rk-section-inner rk-blog-component-layout"><div class="rk-blog-component-copy"><h2>Ready to Storm-Proof Your Roof in Columbus, OH?
