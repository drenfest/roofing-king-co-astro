  <h1>What is covered, by whom, and for how long</h1>
  <p class="lede">There are two separate warranties on a roof and they cover different failures. Most warranty disappointment comes from not knowing which one applies.</p>
</div></section>

<section class="sec"><div class="wrap">
  <div class="prose" style="max-width:76ch">
    <h2>The two kinds of coverage</h2>
    <div class="tblwrap"><table class="tbl">
      <thead><tr><th></th><th>Manufacturer warranty</th><th>Workmanship warranty</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr><td><strong>Who backs it</strong></td><td>GAF</td><td>Roof King &amp; Restoration</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Covers</strong></td><td>Defects in the materials themselves</td><td>Errors in how the roof was installed</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Typical failure</strong></td><td>Granule loss, premature shingle degradation</td><td>Leak at a flashing, fastener placement, ventilation</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Term</strong></td><td>Varies by system and tier</td><td>5-year workmanship warranty</td></tr>
      </tbody>
    </table></div>
    <p>A material defect claim goes to GAF. An installation error comes to us. Contractors who blur that line are usually setting up to point at the other party later.</p>

    <h2>Why Master Elite matters here</h2>
    <p>GAF restricts its strongest warranties to certified contractors. As a Master Elite contractor we can offer the GAF System Plus and Golden Pledge Limited Warranty on qualifying roof systems. A contractor without that certification cannot write them, no matter what their proposal says.</p>
    <p>Golden Pledge in particular includes GAF’s own inspection of the completed installation, which is a meaningful layer of accountability that does not depend on us grading our own work.</p>

    <h2>What voids coverage</h2>
    <ul class="ticks">
      <li><svg width="20" height="20" viewBox="0 0 20 20" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2.2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M4 10.5l4 4 8-9"></path></svg><span><strong>Mixing systems.</strong> Enhanced GAF warranties require qualifying accessory components from the same system. Substituting a cheaper starter or ridge cap to win a bid can disqualify the warranty entirely.</span></li>
      <li><svg width="20" height="20" viewBox="0 0 20 20" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2.2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M4 10.5l4 4 8-9"></path></svg><span><strong>Inadequate ventilation.</strong> Manufacturer coverage generally assumes a properly ventilated attic. A roof installed over a ventilation deficiency can have its coverage challenged.</span></li>
      <li><svg width="20" height="20" viewBox="0 0 20 20" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2.2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M4 10.5l4 4 8-9"></path></svg><span><strong>Later work by others.</strong> A satellite dish, solar array or gutter guard fastened through the roof afterward can affect coverage at that penetration.</span></li>
      <li><svg width="20" height="20" viewBox="0 0 20 20" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2.2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M4 10.5l4 4 8-9"></path></svg><span><strong>Unregistered warranties.</strong> Enhanced manufacturer warranties must be registered. We register yours and give you the documentation rather than telling you it was handled.</span></li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Transferability</h2>
    <p>Enhanced GAF warranties are generally transferable to a subsequent owner within a defined window, which is a genuine asset at resale. Terms and the transfer window are set by GAF and stated in your warranty document.</p>

    <div class="notice"><p><strong>Read your actual warranty document.</strong> What is written on this page is a plain-language summary. The governing terms, exclusions and limits are in the document GAF issues and in your signed agreement with us. If those ever conflict with something a salesperson told you, the document wins. Ask for it in writing before you sign.</p></div>
