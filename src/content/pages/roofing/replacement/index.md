        <p class="rk-home-eyebrow">Repair or replace</p>
        <h2>Know When A Full Roof Replacement Makes Sense</h2>
        <p>Not every roofing issue needs a tear-off. If damage is isolated, a targeted <a href="/roofing/repair/">roof repair</a> may be the smarter path. If leaks keep returning, shingles are failing across multiple slopes, or storm damage has compromised the system, replacement can protect the home more completely.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="rk-page-feature-list">
        <article><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Repair May Fit</h3><p>Small leaks, a few missing shingles, minor flashing issues, or localized wind damage can often be addressed without replacing the entire roof.</p></article>
        <article><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Replacement May Fit</h3><p>Older roofing, widespread granule loss, curling shingles, soft decking, recurring leaks, or damage across several areas can point toward a full system replacement.</p></article>
        <article><span class="rk-card-rule" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>Storm Damage Matters</h3><p>Hail, wind, ice, and heavy Ohio weather can shorten a roof system's life. Our team can inspect the roof and document what we find before you decide.</p></article>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>



<section class="rk-page-section rk-page-copy-section">
    <div class="rk-section-inner rk-page-copy-narrow">
      <p class="rk-home-eyebrow">Warning signs</p>
      <h2>Signs You May Need Roof Replacement</h2>
      <p>Homeowners usually call when a leak becomes obvious, but many replacement clues show up earlier. Watch for missing or lifted shingles, curling edges, bald spots where granules have washed away, dark streaking, repeated repairs, daylight in the attic, soft or sagging decking, damaged flashing, ventilation problems, and roof age that is approaching the end of its expected life.</p>
      <p>A complete inspection helps separate cosmetic wear from problems that can let water reach the decking, insulation, drywall, and interior finishes of your home.</p>
    </div>
  </section>
