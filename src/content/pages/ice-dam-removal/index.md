      <p>During the winter, ice dams can pose a significant hazard to homes. They form when snow melts and refreezes on your roof, typically along gutters. Over time, water can become trapped here and slowly seep into your roof’s substructure, causing severe damage.</p><p>By hiring our team at Roof King &amp; Restoration, you won’t have to worry about ice dams forming any longer. We’re an <a href="/storm-damage/">experienced storm restoration company</a> and ice dam removal contractor in Columbus and with our help, your residence is sure to be protected. We are proudly accredited by the Better Business Bureau with an A+ rating and offer dependable services that all customers can rely on.</p><h2>Prevent Roofing Ice Damage in Columbus<br></h2><p>The most proactive step you can take to prevent snow damage to roofing in Columbus is hiring an expert for ice dam prevention. Here at Roof King, we know exactly how ice dams form and will have no difficulty preventing them on your home. There are a few key ways in which we do this:</p><ul><li><strong>Installing Gutter Guards: </strong>Most ice dams are caused by clogged gutters that do not adequately redirect water. By installing a <a href="/gutters/guards/">new gutter guard system</a>, you won’t have to worry about clogs forming.</li><li><strong>Attic Insulation: </strong>Attic insulation will help reduce the amount of warm air that’s leaving your home through the roof. This will prevent or slow down the rate of snowmelt.</li><li><strong>Roofing Upgrades: </strong>The best way to ensure that your roof is protected is by upgrading it with a new installation and a waterproof underlayment.</li></ul><p>If you already have an ice dam buildup, we can help remove it as well. Our skilled team will be able to precisely thaw the dam and make sure that your roof is completely protected. We can also provide <a href="/roofing/repair/">comprehensive roof repairs</a> in case damage has occurred. As with all our services, we have flexible financing available to help you immediately afford the work you need done.<br></p><h2>Start with a Free Quote to Repair Roofing Ice Damage in Columbus&nbsp;<br></h2><p>Don’t let an ice dam wreak havoc on your home’s roofing and exterior.
