<h2>
        Expert Roofing, Siding, and Gutters Project Near You on Summerfield Dr<br>
    </h2>
<div class="pp-project-details" id="pp-project-details-snip">
            Roof King &amp; Restoration completed a full roof replacement on Summerfield Dr in Lewis Center, Ohio.<br>
High-quality GAF materials and careful installation gave the homeowner stronger protection, improved curb appeal, and greater confidence in performance.
<ul>
<li>Project: Full roofing replacement on Summerfield Dr, Lewis Center, OH</li>
<li>Goal: Stop leaks, improve ventilation, boost curb appeal</li>
<li>Roof replacement: Tear-off, upgraded underlayment, new shingles, ridge ventilation</li>
<li>Outcome: Durable, weather-ready roof backed by warranties</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<h2>Project Overview</h2>
<p>We replaced an aging, storm-damaged roof on Summerfield Dr in Lewis Center, Ohio to restore weather protection and curb appeal. The homeowner wanted a durable, energy-efficient solution after recurring leaks and poor ventilation. Roof King &amp; Restoration installed new shingles, upgraded underlayment and ventilation, improving durability, moisture control and seasonal energy performance across Ohio’s variable climate.<br>
</p>
<h2>Results</h2>
<p>The completed roofing project eliminated persistent leaks and sealed vulnerable roof penetrations, delivering immediate visible improvement in the home’s curb appeal. Upgraded underlayment and attic ventilation reduced moisture buildup and improved thermal performance, helping stabilize indoor temperatures across hot summers and cold winters. The work is backed by the GAF manufacturer limited warranty plus Roof King &amp; Restoration workmanship coverage for homeowner peace of mind.<br>
</p>
<h2>Materials Used</h2>
<p>On Summerfield Dr in Lewis Center, Ohio, Roof King &amp; Restoration completed this roofing project using GAF Timberline HDZ shingles in Charcoal, ABC synthetic underlayment, GAF ice-and-water shield at vulnerable eaves, and a continuous ventilated ridge system to improve airflow. For more information about our roofing services, visit our <a href="/roofing/">Columbus, OH Roofing Company</a>.
        </p></div>
