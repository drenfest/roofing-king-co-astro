<h2>
        Expert Roofing and Siding Project Near You on Country Manor Dr<br>

<div class="pp-project-details" id="pp-project-details-snip">
<h2>Project Overview</h2>
<p>On Country Manor Dr in North Lima, Ohio, Roof King &amp; Restoration replaced an aging, leak-prone shingle roof to restore protection and curb appeal. The homeowner needed better resistance to heavy snow, freeze-thaw cycles, and summer storms. We completed a full tear-off, flashing and ventilation upgrades, and installed high-performance asphalt shingles to improve durability and energy efficiency.</p>
<h2>Results</h2>
<p>This roofing replacement on Country Manor Dr in North Lima, Ohio by Roof King &amp; Restoration resolved persistent leaks and storm damage while meeting local weather demands. Homeowners praised the craftsmanship, clear communication, and the clean, lasting finish delivered by our crew.</p>
<ul>
<li>Project type: Full roof replacement (roofing) on Country Manor Dr, North Lima, OH</li>
<li>Work completed: Tear-off of existing shingles, flashing and underlayment replacement, ventilation improvements, and new high-performance asphalt shingles</li>
<li>Performance gains: Improved weather resistance for Ohio winters, reduced leak risk, and enhanced curb appeal</li>
<li>Warranty and protection: Installed materials include manufacturer warranty and are backed by Roof King &amp; Restoration standard workmanship warranty</li>
<li>Local expertise: For more about our services, see <a href="/roofing/">Columbus, OH Roofing Company</a></li>
</ul></div>
