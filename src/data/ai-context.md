# src/data

product-companions.json owns explicitly verified companion mappings, benefit copy and primary sources; do not broadcast pairings to unverified products. product-pillar.json defines the three hubs; product-details.json defines eleven product page records with stable catalog keys; site-structure.json and page-structure.ts own logical placements and explicit editorial heading overrides. Product and service URL structure are independent. product-catalog.json and product-types.ts hold verified GAF color/spec/warranty records and local image variants; source facts are in migration/sources/gaf-product-catalog.json. service-pages.json holds the migrated eight roofing-family pages and Cleveland roofing and their ordered sections; edit these typed records for native pages. section-types.ts defines the discriminated section and rich-text unions; media.ts merges home/service dimensions and variants. service-media.json is generated by optimize-service-media.mjs. site.json is the authoritative business identity. home.json contains WordPress homepage content; navigation.json contains menu/link data. home-media.json maps original media to dimensions and optimized WebP variants in public/media/home. roofr.json holds only the public estimator URL and calculator route list; credentials belong in deployment secrets. imported-shell.json remains only for legacy pages. Do not treat imported claims as fact-checked.
