# src/data

review-platforms.json maps review-source names to authentic sized logos; Birdeye SVG comes from its public review-profile header. featured-reviews.json owns the selected third-party review excerpts, exact display names, original URLs, verification date and evidence notes. Home, roofing-landing and service-pages review sections select reviewIds; imported first-party review text remains only in migration evidence. Keep numeric ratings absent unless verified for that individual review, and distinguish excerpts from editorial headings. roofing-landing.json is the current editorial /roofing/ copy, imagery, credentials, review attribution and FAQs. Residential tab previews reflect their linked detail pages; reviews and credentials precede materials, and the diagnostic checklist is omitted. The older service-pages /roofing/ sections are evidence except its active brandIds selection. Products navigation is derived from product-pillar/product-details breadcrumbs by scripts/sync-product-navigation.mjs; rerun it after hierarchy additions to synchronize navigation.json and imported-shell.json. brand-library.json defines stable brand IDs, names, sized logos and material categories; BrandSection brandIds in home/service/product-pillar data control exactly which records render and in what order. The live metal page names GAF; see migration/brand-inventory.md for evidence and brand/product-line distinctions. product-energy.json stores dated manufacturer energy values for exact product/color/plant/CRRC listings; never apply them across colors or imply percentage savings. product-companions.json owns explicitly verified companion mappings, benefit copy and primary sources; do not broadcast pairings to unverified products. brand-pages.json maps logo names to internal brand/certification routes; product-pillar.json defines eleven category/material hubs and seven brand pages; product-details.json defines eleven product page records with stable catalog keys; site-structure.json and page-structure.ts own logical placements and explicit editorial heading overrides. Product and service URL structure are independent. product-catalog.json and product-types.ts hold verified GAF color/spec/warranty records and local image variants; source facts are in migration/sources/gaf-product-catalog.json. service-pages.json holds the migrated eight roofing-family pages, Cleveland roofing and the commercial roofing hub and their ordered sections; edit these typed records for native pages. section-types.ts defines the discriminated section and rich-text unions; media.ts merges home/service/brand dimensions and variants. service-media.json is generated by optimize-service-media.mjs. site.json is the authoritative business identity. home.json contains WordPress homepage content; navigation.json contains menu/link data. home-media.json maps original media to dimensions and optimized WebP variants in public/media/home. roofr.json holds only the public estimator URL and calculator route list; credentials belong in deployment secrets. imported-shell.json remains only for legacy pages. Do not treat imported claims as fact-checked.
