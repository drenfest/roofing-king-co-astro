The biggest win

Use /roofing/ to finish the shared service system. Apply it to the rest of the service family, then build two location variants. Share structure, media handling and behavior while keeping the service/city copy specific.

The homepage is the design reference. The homepage and all eight roofing-family URLs are native today; the two calculator pages have a native Roofr estimator inside the imported page.

Reuse has limits

Projects, financing offers and full reviews currently inherit a blog wrapper. Give them purpose-specific record templates. Missing team/vacancy/event content and unconnected tools require real data—not extra generic proof sections.

Supporting pages stay accessible and useful. Prioritization should come from the navigation and relevant internal links, with no automatic deletion, redirect or deindexing.

Data drives the sections

Each page retains its permalink, canonical, title and description. It selects a family plus ordered typed sections, with references to shared services, locations, projects, offers and reviews. This lets us improve a section once without changing every page’s content or URL.

Use Astro for heroes, copy, cards, FAQs, maps and listings. Use Svelte for navigation, gallery filters/lightbox, image comparison and interactive forms/tools. Keep a single quote modal host in the shared shell.

Zapier delivery is deferred. Jared Lane remains the confirmed lead owner. Custom quote submission stays disabled until delivery is verified.
