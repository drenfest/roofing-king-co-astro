#### Include

### What’s Included in Our James Hardie Services

Our James Hardie siding services include removal of old siding (if needed), a full wall inspection and preparation, and professional installation of Hardie siding, trim, and soffits. We install proper moisture barriers and flashing for long-term protection and offer design consultations to help you choose the right styles and colors. Every project ends with a full cleanup and final inspection.
