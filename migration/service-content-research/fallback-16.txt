L7: 
L8: When a storm hits, roof damage is not always easy to spot from the ground. We help homeowners in Delaware County, OH find the problem fast, stop active leaks, and make a clear plan for repair. At Myers Roofing And Siding, storm damage repair means more than patching a few shingles. We inspect the full roofing system, look for hidden damage, and guide you through what needs to happen next so you can protect your home and avoid bigger problems.
L9: Storms can damage a roof in several ways. High winds can lift or tear off shingles. Hail can bruise roofing materials, crack shingle surfaces, and shorten the life of the roof. Heavy rain can push water under damaged areas and lead to leaks, wood rot, mold, or ceiling stains. Siding and gutters can also take a hit during severe weather, which is why we look at the exterior as a whole instead of focusing on one visible issue.
L10: ## What our storm damage repair service includes
L11: We start with a detailed inspection of the roof, flashing, vents, ridge caps, valleys, gutters, and siding. We check for missing shingles, lifted tabs, hail impact marks, granule loss, soft spots, punctures, dented metal components, and water entry points. If there is interior leaking, we also pay attention to signs like wet insulation, attic moisture, and staining on walls or ceilings. This helps us understand both the direct damage and any secondary issues caused by the storm.
L12: After the inspection, we explain what we found in simple terms. If the damage is minor, we may recommend targeted roof repairs to restore the affected area and help extend the life of the existing roof. If the storm damage is widespread or the roofing system has been compromised beyond a practical repair, we will explain that clearly and discuss replacement options. Our goal is to give you honest guidance based on the condition of your home, not a one-size-fits-all sales pitch.

L22: ## Why fast storm damage repair matters
L23: 
L24: Even small storm damage can turn into a much larger repair if it is left alone. A loose shingle can lead to a leak. A leak can soak the roof deck, attic insulation, drywall, and framing. Fast action helps lower the risk of more costly structural damage and can improve the chances of a smoother insurance claim by documenting the condition early.
