L154: ## Preventing Future Ice Dam Problems
L155: 
L156: Removing the ice is only half the solution; preventing it from coming back is just as important. After completing your ice dam removal, we inspect your roof and attic to pinpoint what caused the problem, such as inadequate insulation, poor ventilation, or warm air leaks into the attic. These issues allow snow to melt unevenly and refreeze at the roof edge.
